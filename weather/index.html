Weather basics

Temperature – how hot or cold the air is. "Feels like" also counts wind and humidity.

Humidity – the amount of water vapour in the air. High humidity makes hot days feel heavier.

Wind speed – air moving from high pressure to low pressure. Measured in km/h.

Pressure – the weight of the air above us. Falling pressure usually means rain or storms; rising pressure means clear skies.

UV index – strength of sunlight. 0–2 low, 3–5 moderate, 6–7 high, 8+ very high (use sunscreen).

Precipitation – any water falling from clouds: rain, drizzle, snow or hail.

Weather vs climate – weather is what happens today; climate is the average weather over 30 or more years.
